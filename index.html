BEHIND THE MUSIC

Meet Jason

A songwriter finding his way back to the music he loves.

Jason Sedgwick writes country music rooted in real life: the wrong turns, fresh starts, lasting friendships, and small moments that mean everything.

After years away from singing, Jason returned to writing and recording original songs, drawing inspiration from classic country storytelling and his own life experiences.

His debut album project, Somewhere Past Yesterday, brings those stories together—one song at a time.

Dogs have always held a special place in Jason’s heart. His girl, Reilly Rose, came into his life after her previous owner, who had cared for her for nine months, was no longer able to keep her. Five years later, Reilly Rose remains a cherished companion and a reminder of the difference a loving home can make.

JASON SEDGWICK ✦

BEHIND THE MUSIC · ORIGINAL SONG STORY

RELEASED

Wrong Side of the Tracks

For me, Wrong Side of the Tracks is about the challenges we face in everyday life and the choices we make along the way.

There are times when it feels like you've missed your train, taken the wrong direction, or ended up on the wrong side of the tracks no matter how hard you've tried. Sometimes, just when you're making progress, there are people ready to cut you down instead of celebrating how far you've come.

The image of railroad tracks stretching for miles has always stayed with me. Empty miles, long roads, short roads—sometimes the journey can feel endless, even when you're trying to find your way forward.

I wrote this song to put those feelings into words, but also to remind myself that the road doesn't have to end where things went wrong. There's still a chance to keep moving.

— Jason Sedgwick Original Songwriter | Jason Sedgwick Music

BEHIND THE MUSIC · ORIGINAL SONG STORY

UPCOMING SINGLE

Borrowed Time

As I've gotten older, I've noticed how much faster time seems to pass. I may not feel much older on the inside, but the years have a way of reminding me that nothing stays the same forever.

Borrowed Time came from thinking about the people and companions I love most—especially my mother and my dog—and how hard it is to imagine a day when they might not be here. Tomorrow isn't promised to any of us, and that makes the time we do have together worth so much more.

I sometimes wish I'd started writing and producing music twenty years ago. But I also realize that the experiences I've had since then are part of what gives these songs their meaning.

For me, this song is a reminder to appreciate every day God gives us, hold on to the people we love, and say the things that matter while we still have the chance.

— Jason Sedgwick Original Songwriter | Jason Sedgwick Music

BEHIND THE MUSIC · ORIGINAL SONG STORY

IN DEVELOPMENT · UNRELEASED

I Ain't Done Yet

For nearly 20 years, law enforcement was a major part of my life. It wasn't just a career; it was part of who I was. When I had to leave the profession earlier than I expected, I found myself facing something I never imagined. I had to start over, reinvent myself, and figure out what came next.

Starting over wasn't easy. There were setbacks, difficult decisions, and times when I questioned whether I would ever find my way forward again.

I could have simply accepted that the best years of my life were behind me. I could have stopped trying and allowed my circumstances to determine my future.

But I wasn't ready to do that.

I decided to return to school and pursue my bachelor's degree in computer science, with plans to continue toward my master's. Along the way, I rediscovered something I had lost many years earlier: my love for writing music.

I started writing songs again, finding inspiration in the experiences, challenges, and lessons that had shaped my life.

I also started my own business focused on technology, research, and development, with the hope of using computational science to support medical research into diseases such as cancer and ALS. I'm also working toward establishing a nonprofit organization dedicated to making a difference.

None of these paths comes with a guarantee. I don't know where they'll lead, and I'm still figuring things out as I go.

But that's part of life.

You can't fail at something you never give yourself the chance to try.

That's what inspired me to write I Ain't Done Yet.

It's not a song about having everything figured out. It's about getting knocked down, finding the determination to get back up, and refusing to let setbacks define who you are.

And when it comes to my music, I want to make something clear. I'm not doing this because I expect to become famous or some big country music superstar. I'm doing it because I love it. Writing music has become a form of therapy for me, a way to put my thoughts, feelings, and experiences into something meaningful.

These songs come from my life. They're personal, they're real, and they're a part of who I am. If someone listens to one of my songs and finds something they can relate to, something that reminds them of their own experiences or helps them through a difficult time, then that means more to me than any amount of recognition.

I may not be where I thought I'd be at this point in my life, but I'm still moving forward, still learning, and still chasing new dreams.

I don't know exactly where this journey will take me.

But one thing I do know is that I ain't done yet.

— Jason Sedgwick Original Songwriter | Jason Sedgwick Music

BEHIND THE MUSIC · ORIGINAL SONG STORY

WRITTEN · UPCOMING RELEASE

Country Just Ain't Country Anymore

Growing up, I listened to some of the greatest voices country music has ever known. Artists like Conway Twitty, George Jones, George Strait, and Alan Jackson helped shape my love for country music. I also became a huge fan of Toby Keith and Brooks & Dunn.

There was something special about the music back then. The unmistakable sound of a steel guitar, the emotion in a singer's voice, and songs that told stories about real life. Love, heartbreak, loss, hard times, and sometimes losing just about everything you had.

Country music was heartfelt and honest, and that's what made it special to me.

Over the years, country music has changed. It's evolved, blending with other genres and introducing some incredibly talented performers along the way. I have nothing against those artists or their talent. Many of them are amazing at what they do.

But somewhere along the way, I feel like we've lost a little of what made country music country.

The steel guitar that once helped define the sound has become less prominent in much of today's mainstream country music. And those heartfelt stories about a man losing his job, his home, his dog, and the love of his life don't seem to have quite the same place they once did.

That's what inspired me to write Country Just Ain't Country Anymore.

This song isn't about saying that today's music is bad or that country music shouldn't evolve. Music will always change, and that's part of its beauty.

It's about remembering the music I grew up loving, the artists who influenced me, and the sound that made country music feel like home.

I don't believe that sound needs to disappear just because the genre continues to evolve.

Sometimes, I just miss hearing that steel guitar cry.

— Jason Sedgwick Original Songwriter | Jason Sedgwick Music

BEHIND THE MUSIC · ORIGINAL SONG STORY

UNRELEASED

Footprints

Many people are familiar with the Christian poem Footprints in the Sand. It tells the story of someone looking back over their life and realizing that during their most difficult moments, when they could no longer stand on their own, the Lord was carrying them.

That message has taken on a much deeper meaning for me as I've gotten older and experienced some of life's challenges.

There have been times in my life when I've questioned my direction, my purpose, and even my faith in God. Times when it seemed like no matter how hard I tried, I couldn't seem to find my way forward.

And the truth is, I'm still finding my way.

I haven't reached some magical point where everything has fallen into place. I still face challenges, uncertainties, and moments when I wonder where any of the roads I'm traveling will lead.

But when I look back at some of the most difficult chapters of my life, I believe it was only by the grace of God that I made it through them.

Even during the times when I struggled with my faith, I believe He never stopped carrying me.

That's what inspired me to write Footprints.

To me, this song is about faith, perseverance, and the strength we sometimes find when we feel we have none left.

I've learned that success isn't necessarily measured by fame, money, or recognition. Sometimes success is simply having the courage to get up another day, continue working toward something you believe in, and refuse to give up, even when it feels like the whole world is against you.

I don't know whether the paths I've chosen will lead to everything I hope to accomplish. Maybe they will, maybe they won't.

But I do know that the journey itself has meaning.

And I believe that when we reach those moments when we feel completely alone, when there's nowhere left to turn and no one seems to believe in our dreams, we may discover that we aren't as alone as we think.

For me, that source of strength is God. For someone else, it may be their own faith or spiritual beliefs.

Sometimes all it takes is reaching out and believing that something greater than ourselves is helping us find the strength to take another step.

I may not be where I want to be yet.

But I believe the Lord is still carrying me, and someday I'll be strong enough to walk those footprints beside Him again.

— Jason Sedgwick Original Songwriter | Jason Sedgwick Music

BEHIND THE MUSIC · ORIGINAL SONG STORY

UNRELEASED

Somewhere Between Never and Forever

When I was just 13 years old, my family experienced a loss that would change our lives forever. My father passed away at only 42 years old, leaving my mother, who was not quite 40, to raise three children on her own.

Rather than focusing on finding someone new, my mother devoted herself to her children and building a life for our family. She put her own needs aside in many ways to make sure we had what we needed.

For more than three decades, that was her life.

Then, 33 years later, something unexpected happened.

At 73 years old, my mother met someone. They began dating, built a relationship, and four years later decided to celebrate their love and commitment to one another with a commitment ceremony.

Not a traditional marriage, but something that means just as much to them.

Watching my mother find companionship again after so many years reminded me that love doesn't have an expiration date. It doesn't always happen when we expect it, and sometimes it arrives when we aren't even looking for it.

My own experiences with love have been a little different.

I've had relationships, experienced heartbreak, and even spent five years engaged before that chapter of my life came to an end.

When I was younger, I didn't always handle heartbreak particularly well. I believed in finding that one person to share my life with, and when relationships ended, I sometimes struggled to accept it.

But as I've gotten older, my perspective has changed.

Today, I'm genuinely happy being single. I've learned to appreciate my independence, focus on my own goals, and find happiness in the life I'm building.

Of course, there are moments when it would be nice to have someone to share life's joys, disappointments, and everyday experiences with.

But I've also learned something from watching my mother.

You don't have to spend your life searching for someone else to make it complete.

Sometimes the best thing you can do is focus on yourself, build the life you want, and let the future unfold in its own time.

That's what inspired me to write Somewhere Between Never and Forever.

It's about the uncertainty of love, the possibility of finding it when you least expect it, and learning that being alone doesn't necessarily mean being unhappy.

Maybe someday I'll meet someone who changes the way I feel about love again.

Maybe I won't.

And for the first time in my life, I'm comfortable with either possibility.

Because somewhere between never and forever, there's a whole lot of life worth living.

— Jason Sedgwick Original Songwriter | Jason Sedgwick Music

MUSIC WITH A PURPOSE

Giving Back to the Dogs Who Need Us

A love of dogs. A second chance. A mission in the making.

Reilly Rose became part of Jason’s life when her previous owner could no longer care for her after nine months. Jason has now shared five years with his girl, and that bond is part of what inspires his commitment to helping dogs find safety, care, and loving homes.

Jason is currently working to establish K9 Innovative Solutions (K9IS) as a future nonprofit organization. The vision is to create a free, technology-driven platform that helps connect rescues and shelters with potential adopters, foster families, volunteers, transporters, and supporters. The goal is to improve coordination, expand visibility for dogs in need, and support better rescue outcomes.

A portion of any music royalties Jason receives will be donated to support K9 Innovative Solutions as the organization develops. The nonprofit is still in the process of being established; this is a commitment for future royalties, not a claim that K9IS already has tax-exempt status.
